Restricts which email domains may sign in through SSO. Enter one or more domains separated by commas or spaces, for example example.com or example.com, example.org. A leading @ is optional.

After Microsoft verifies the user's identity, their email / UPN domain is checked against this list. A user whose domain is not listed is rejected even if they are individually provisioned or a member of an allowed group — a defence against guest or cross-tenant accounts gaining access through group membership.

Leave blank to allow any domain (the default). This gate is separate from the provisioning portal's allowed-domains setting, which controls who may request access.
